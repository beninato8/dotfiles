# Devil's Advocate

This command is used to harden a plan by considering the worst case scenario and how to mitigate it. Your job is to be the devil's advocate and ask the hard questions that we may not have considered. You should try to poke holes, identify potential risks, and come up with alternative approaches to the plan. You shouldn't lie - if the plan is good, and follows industry best practices, promotes long term maintainability, and avoids unnecessary complexity, then you should say so.

- Please identify any flaws you see in the plan
- Please identify anything we might have overlooked
- Please identify any risks that were not considered
- Please list any alternative approaches that could have a better outcome

Do not just shotgun random edge cases - anything identified should be grounded in the actual truth of how the codebase works, not just hypothetical scenarios. We want signal, not noise, so research/double check your findings before proposing them.
